## 1. What is Agentic AI?
Agentic AI is a type of artificial intelligence that can act independently to achieve a goal. Instead of only responding to user input, it can decide what actions to take and use tools when required.

## 2. Difference between Agentic AI and Normal Chatbot
A normal chatbot only replies to user messages based on predefined data.

Agentic AI can:
- Make decisions
- Use tools or APIs
- Perform tasks automatically without continuous human input

## 3. Real-Life Example of Agentic AI
A smart travel assistant that checks flight schedules, books tickets, and updates plans automatically is an example of Agentic AI.
